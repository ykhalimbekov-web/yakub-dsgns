About me

I’m Yokubbek (Jacob), a 27-year-old graphic designer from Tashkent. My goal is to create meaningful, impactful design that tells stories, evokes emotion and connects with people on a deeper level — visuals that not only look great but leave a lasting impression.

I work across branding, packaging and art direction, from private-label systems for large retail chains to identities for small businesses.

Branding

Identity work starts with immersion in the brand and its market. From there I define the visual direction, build the corporate identity and bring it together in a brand book — so the brand gets not a set of separate elements, but a coherent visual system ready to grow.

Packaging

Packaging design starts with the product, its audience and the shelf it competes on. I develop the concept, structure and graphics, then prepare print-ready layouts for every SKU — packaging that stands out, communicates clearly and scales across the whole range.

Arts

Beyond commercial briefs, I work on illustration, art direction and visual experiments. These projects are a space to test new forms, colour and techniques — ideas that later feed back into branding and packaging work.
