Iz Korzinki

Rebranding / Packaging Design

The project:

It is a proprietary trademark of the Korzinka market chain, created for customers who value high quality, reliability and confidence in their choice. The brand offers both food and non-food products, being a worthy alternative to A-brands, but at the same time remaining affordable.

Skills acquired:

  • Brand Identity
  • Packaging design
  • Market analysis

Future plans

In the next 1–2 years, the “Iz Korzinki” brand is aimed at:

  • Expansion of the product range due to new product categories, especially in the segment of “household goods” and “functional nutrition”;
  • Deepening differentiation from other PL brands through unique packaging formats and additional visual elements;
  • Strengthening partnerships with local manufacturers who adhere to high standards;
  • Increase brand awareness through outdoor advertising, digital campaigns and sales floor promotion;
  • In-depth analytics of customer behavior to more accurately adapt the product line to the preferences of the key audience.
