Lama

Rebranding / Packaging Design

The project:

Packaging for Lama, a plant-based brand of cutlets and nuggets in the mid-price segment, developed with the CIS market, especially Uzbekistan, in mind.

Skills acquired:

  • Brand Identity
  • Packaging design
  • Market analysis

Project goal

Create packaging for vegan delicacies that attracts the attention of a mid-price audience, taking into account the specifics of the CIS market (especially Uzbekistan).

Objectives

Stand out from competitors with an innovative design. Reflect the brand values: naturalness, eco-friendliness, convenience. Develop packaging concepts, including flexible and rigid solutions.

Market analysis (semi-finished products)

Main observations

When analysing packaging of various products on supermarket chiller shelves, several common features were identified:

Bright colours: almost all packs use bright, saturated shades that create a positive mood and catch the shopper’s eye.

Structured elements: packs organise their visual elements: first a large, bold logo (which identifies the brand instantly, especially on marketplaces), then a product-name zone, often accompanied by a product image. The photo plays a key role in making the product look appetising and trustworthy.

Conclusions and proposals

Main advantages of the packaging:

Eco-friendliness, visual appeal, convenience. The packaging emphasises the naturalness and benefits of the product.

Potential for rollout:

Huge demand for functional and eco-friendly solutions. A chance to differentiate through an innovative approach.

Recommendations for further development:

  • Develop a series of packs for different types of delicacies: cutlets, dumplings and sauces.
  • Introduce refill solutions for regular customers.
  • Test the packaging on the target audience in the CIS.
